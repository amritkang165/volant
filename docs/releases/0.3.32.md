## Changed

- **AI Chat is ready to type.** Tab from search connects right away and picks up your last conversation; New in the header still starts a fresh one. If you haven't chosen a provider yet, the chat lists the coding agents installed on your Mac, and one click connects to the one you pick instead of opening Settings. (#352)
- **Agent replies show tables and nested lists.** Markdown tables render as a grid with their column alignment, numbered steps keep their sub-bullets and numbering, and copying a table gives tab-separated rows. The Notes preview gets the same improvements. (#355)
- **Clicking away hides the launcher everywhere.** Translate and AI Chat no longer stay on screen; Translate comes back with your text on the next summon, and AI Chat keeps its conversation for Tab. (#352)
- **Escape steps back first in AI Chat.** The first Escape returns to search with the conversation and your draft kept; a second closes Volant. (#352)
- **Translate starts fresh after you dismiss it.** Escape, Back or the shortcut clear the text and result, so the next summon opens search instead of yesterday's translation. (#352)
- **Cleaner Translate and AI Chat.** The white dividers and boxes around them are gone. (#352)
- **Star Volant on GitHub** from Settings › About. (#349)

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Dictation requires macOS 26.
