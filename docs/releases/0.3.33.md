## New

- **Your Volant.** A new section in Settings shows your own launcher activity: choices over the last 30 days with a bar for each day, active days, your current streak and busiest day, and the apps, commands and extensions you use most lately. It's counted only on your Mac and never sent anywhere; Volant still has no telemetry. Clear Activity History resets the daily counts whenever you like. (#357)

Daily counts start with this version, so the chart fills in from today.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Dictation requires macOS 26.
